## Highest-value gaps (capabilities, ranked)

These are what leadership still cannot do in 5 seconds on the combined page.

1. **Exception briefing (inverted pyramid)**  
   Open on “what needs me this month,” not 17 equal cards. Hero: off-track and at-risk only, with owner and vs-plan vs-peer in one line. On-track stays one click away under All. This matches the notes: flags only when below benchmark.

2. **Strategy map (cause-and-effect)**  
   The BSC *is* the map, not the KPI grid. [`executive_bsc_dashboard.html`](c:\Users\TBaldwin\OneDrive - Jack Henry\Desktop\Executive Dashboarding\executive_bsc_dashboard.html) already has this; the combined monitor does not. Bank vs CU maps differ at the top (ROA/ROE vs member value + net worth). Without links, L&D and Process look like HR/ops reports instead of leading indicators of earnings and capital.

3. **Third drill: drivers + next action**  
   Notes said max **3** drills. Combined monitor stops at (1) KPI & gaps and (2) Trend & peers. Missing: why it missed (2–4 drivers) and what we are doing (initiative, owner, due date). That is the board conversation. ROA tornado in the executive mockup is the pattern — do not invent a fourth level.

4. **Period context executives always ask**  
   As-of date, YTD vs plan, vs prior year. Monthly sparkline vs a static target is not enough for ALCO or the board packet.

5. **Definition lock on the card**  
   One-line formula + source (UBPR vs FPR). Prevents “whose efficiency?” and “are we grading the CU on a bank curve?” — already a documented failure mode.

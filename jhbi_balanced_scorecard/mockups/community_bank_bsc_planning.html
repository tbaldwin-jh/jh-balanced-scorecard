FY2026 plan cycle · Sample
How to enter a plan. Work the steps in order for each objective you own. (1) Create or update the objective (name, perspective, owner, intent). (2) Bind the proving metric and lock its formula and source inputs. (3) Enter the bank plan target or limit and cadence. (4) Attach the peer benchmark (peer set, median or percentile, as-of date). (5) Run calc QA and confirm engine value matches your source-grain replay. (6) Publish only after QA passes so the executive scorecard shows Actual, Target, and Peer correctly. Recommended control loop

Order Action Exit criteria
1 Enter / refresh objectives by BSC perspective Owner, description, and success intent clear
2 Bind metric(s) and lock formula + inputs Single dictionary definition; no competing formulas
3 Set plan target or limit Numeric target with cadence
4 Attach peer benchmark Peer set, percentile, and as-of date documented
5 Run calc QA (engine vs replay) Match within tolerance; definition notes filed
6 Publish to executive scorecard Appears as Actual / Target / Peer on president view
